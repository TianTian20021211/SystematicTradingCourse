# Papers

The PDFs are kept locally and are **not committed** to this public repository.
Use the citations below to retrieve them through the library or the course.

## Core references

1. Paschke, R., Prokopczuk, M., & Wese Simen, C. (2020).
   *Curve Momentum.* Journal of Banking & Finance, 113, 105718.
   - Within-curve momentum; first two nearbys; 1-month holding.
   - Rolls the first nearby at the end of the second month before the last
     trading day; uses the `(i+1)`-th nearby price on roll days.

2. Lubnau, T., & Todorova, N. (2015).
   *Trading on Mean-Reversion in Energy Futures Markets.*
   Energy Economics, 51, 312–319.
   - Kalman-filtered hedge ratio; Bollinger bands on the hedged spread.
   - Front-month vs 2–5 month futures; 2–21 day holding periods.

3. Bianchi, R. J., Fan, J. H., Miffre, J., & Zhang, T. (2023).
   *Exploiting the Dynamics of Commodity Futures Curves.*
   Journal of Banking & Finance, 154, 106965.
   - Nelson–Siegel fit to the four nearest contracts; slope/butterfly spreads.
   - Holds contracts until the last trading day before the front contract
     enters its maturity month.

## Additional references

4. Boons, M., & Porras Prado, M. (2019). *Basis-Momentum.*
   Journal of Finance, 74(1), 239–279.
   - Local file: `basis_momentum.pdf`.
   - The difference between first- and second-nearby momentum; it subsumes
     basis and conventional momentum in joint tests.
   - Used as the theoretical/empirical basis for idea D (the within-commodity
     signal is mathematically basis-momentum).
5. Hong, S., & Klabjan, D. (2026). Graph learning for calendar spreads.
   (Not in the local folder; cited in the proposal.)
